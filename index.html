Install this app on your phone

Add it to your home screen so it opens full screen like an app.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this app’s web address in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Confirm. The icon appears on your home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this app’s web address in Safari (not Chrome or another app’s browser).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

You will only see this once. You can open it again from the Guide tab.
